## Port bank and numbering

Logical Port cells are separate top-level numbered rectangles inside the AP block, with solid fill, consistent dimensions and one horizontal row. Their actual text is1–24, all unique and complete. Neither XML order nor Shape ID arithmetic supplies a Port number. Capacity is independently extracted from the block callout. Numeric Fiber labels (e.g.Shape16006) and green elliptical Fiber markers (e.g.Shape15997) are negative fixtures and cannot become Ports or occupancy records.

## Fiber binding and drawing tolerance

Cable marker banks come from the already decoded source construction. Explicit Fiber-number labels in each bank are matched locally to circular Fiber markers. The number row may lie above or below markers; Tube-number callouts outside that row cannot supply Fiber numbers. The resulting numbering independently agrees with all 2302 certified transition-marker endpoints. Color is descriptive identity, never a binding key.

For each AP, only banks on its source-labeled Cable and within its block footprint are eligible. The closest eligible endpoint bank must be vertically distinct from another bank. Every Port then requires a unique numbered marker under a local X tolerance of0.28×the smaller of the Port/marker pitches, row tolerance0.003, and one drawn connector linking the Port's top edge to that marker bank. Port-end Y tolerance is0.02; marker-end Y tolerance0.05 in original Visio inches, bounded by local marker diameter and spacing. All source Ports satisfy this repeated, nonoverlapping bank pattern. AP42 pitch is approximately0.090864; its X tolerance is0.025442. Small actual drawing offsets are retained, not rounded away. Competing markers/connectors or missing cells produce review rather than synthesis. No global nearest-neighbor or24=24 count equality rule is used.

Each pair retains observed/expected coordinates, delta, competing candidates, pitches, bank IDs, connector IDs, source Shape IDs and number-label evidence in source-b-ap-port-audit.json. No splice/patch/jumper operation is asserted.
