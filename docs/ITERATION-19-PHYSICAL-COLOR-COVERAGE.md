## Decoder boundary

The confirmed source convention is applied only to the repeated circular marker structure:

- outer ring = Tube physical color;
- inner fill = Fiber physical color;
- each repeated 12-position group establishes one Tube/module;
- paired marker banks must agree;
- connector and cable line color is ignored;
- unsupported marker structures or raw values remain unknown.

The bounded raw map adds three source encodings found on the larger banks: `#7f7f7f` = slate, `#7030a0` = violet and `#f9bb00` = orange. Individual rings that differ from the dominant repeated ring within a 12-position group remain recorded as warnings while group identity is derived from the repeated peer rings. Fiber and Tube identity never comes from route-line styling.
