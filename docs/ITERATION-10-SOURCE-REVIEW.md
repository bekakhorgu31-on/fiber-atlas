## Tsageri source convention, version 1

This rule applies only to this reviewed Tsageri–Lentekhi source:

1. The yellow rectangle is the aggregation context, not the ODF itself.
2. The ODF is a separate contained logical asset.
3. Internal ODF port graphics and lower-boundary port markers are alternative representations of the same logical ports.
4. The deduplication key is `ODF-TSAGERI-01/{logical-port-number}`.
5. Outgoing lines leave through the lower boundary. Cable labels may sit outside the yellow rectangle.
6. Geometry, style, adjacency and shared color are evidence only. They cannot silently approve a cable, fiber or optical association.
7. Dense connector bands contain auxiliary or edited shapes. Their raw count is not used as ODF capacity.

No panel or module split is visible with enough certainty, so the reviewed hierarchy is `Aggregation 5 → ODF-TSAGERI-01 → Port 1…192`. A future panel import can add containment without changing Port identity.
